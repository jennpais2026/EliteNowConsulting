Common Questions

What ServiceNow Leaders Ask Us First

What does a ServiceNow consulting partner do?

A ServiceNow consulting partner helps enterprises implement, configure, and optimize the ServiceNow platform. At EliteNow Consulting, we specialize in ITSM, NowAssist, CMDB, IRM, and ITOM — architecting workflows that drive efficiency, reduce technical debt, and ensure the platform delivers the results you were promised.

How long does a ServiceNow ITSM implementation take?

Most enterprise ITSM implementations take 12 weeks under EliteNow's Configuration First methodology. We deliver in 2-week sprints with Friday demos so you see progress, blockers, and results in real time — not just at the end.

What is ServiceNow technical debt and why does it matter?

Technical debt is the accumulation of custom scripts, hard-coded values, and non-standard configurations that make ServiceNow harder to upgrade and maintain. It quietly raises your total cost of ownership, blocks new features, and slows the platform down. EliteNow's Platform Governance practice identifies, prioritizes, and remediates technical debt to restore Out-Of-The-Box (OOTB) alignment.

What does OOTB Restoration mean?

OOTB Restoration means systematically replacing heavy customizations with native, configurable ServiceNow features. The goal is a platform that upgrades cleanly, performs well, and adopts new ServiceNow capabilities (like NowAssist) without rework. We configure first and customize only when absolutely necessary.

Do you work with small and mid-sized businesses?

Yes. EliteNow Consulting is a boutique practice focused on small-to-medium businesses that want senior expertise without the overhead of a Big-Four-style engagement. You get direct access to a senior ServiceNow architect, strategic partnership, and accountability for outcomes — not just technical go-live dates.

What ServiceNow modules do you implement?

We specialize in ITSM, Platform Governance, Strategic Integrations (Azure AD, Jira, Slack, Okta, ServiceNow IntegrationHub), and Process Automation (Flow Designer, Virtual Agent, NowAssist, Predictive Intelligence). We also work with CMDB, IRM, and ITOM.
