Platform Governance

Technical debt audits, upgrade readiness assessments, and Out-Of-The-Box (OOTB) restoration.

Restore Your Platform's Health

Over time, complex enterprise environments accumulate technical debt. Custom scripts, hard-coded values, and non-standard architecture choices may solve immediate problems, but they create long-term fragility. This leads to painful upgrades, slow performance, and a platform that feels like a liability rather than an asset.

At EliteNow Consulting, our Platform Governance services are designed to audit your current state and map a rigorous path back to Out-Of-The-Box (OOTB) alignment.

Our Governance Capabilities

  • Technical Debt Audits: Comprehensive instance reviews identifying rogue scripts, failing integrations, and customized workflows dragging down performance.
  • Upgrade Readiness: Pre-upgrade testing and remediation to ensure you can adopt the newest ServiceNow releases without breaking your business processes.
  • OOTB Restoration: Methodical replacement of heavy customizations with native, configurable ServiceNow features to lower your Total Cost of Ownership.
  • Best Practice Frameworks: Implementation of strict coding standards, System Property usage, and release management protocols for your internal teams.
